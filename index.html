Why it matters

1 site

Concentrated animals, concentrated emissions

A CAFO houses thousands of animals at one site. Feed is brought in, and manure accumulates in lagoons, pits and litter, where it releases methane and nitrous oxide.

≈ 32 %

Methane: strong and fast-acting

Livestock accounts for about a third of human-caused methane. Over 20 years, methane traps about 80 times more heat than CO₂.

≈ 273×

Nitrogen losses

Only part of the protein that animals eat is retained in milk, meat or eggs. The rest is excreted and lost as ammonia, nitrate and nitrous oxide (N₂O), a greenhouse gas about 273 times stronger than CO₂ over 100 years.

Measure before you mitigate

The simulator follows every flow through a farm, from feed, water and energy in to products, manure and gases out. That shows where the footprint comes from before mitigation options are compared.
